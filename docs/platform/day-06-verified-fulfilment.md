Permanent mismatches and unrelated signed payment events with no order ID are durably recorded as ignored. Invalid signatures return 401. Provider errors, unattached order IDs, lagging capture and transaction errors return 503 for retry. A processed legacy receipt with no order and no outcome is classified as replayable. It remains in place until an authentic retry supplies enough evidence to bind and process it in the fulfilment transaction. Already completed receipts remain idempotent. A refund or dispute notification bound to the payment and order can suspend guest access while verification is pending. The receipt remains retryable if the provider is unavailable.

## Proposed V1 Test Mode entitlement policy

A matching capture activates publication. Authorized, failed, and cancelled payments carry no publication entitlement. Full refunds revoke access. Partial refunds and unresolved refund or dispute evidence suspend access. The owner retains the invitation and reserved slug. The guest page, social metadata, and protected cover consult current entitlement. A guest request that starts after the entitlement transaction commits is denied. A request already reading the prior committed snapshot may finish.

The fulfilment RPC preserves original capture evidence when entitlement changes. Owner status requests can recheck verified payments. A signed or repeated capture cannot reactivate an entitlement after suspension or revocation. There is no automatic or customer-facing reactivation path. Any future reactivation after suspension needs an explicit, authorized policy and audited operator procedure. No refund is initiated by YUGMA. Founder approval of refund, dispute, and reinstatement policy is required before commercial launch.

On checkout restart, the server reads the provider order’s payment attempts and rechecks whether that same order can be opened under the database lock. Matching late authorization moves a failed receipt to processing. Existing processing cannot reopen by elapsed time alone. Subsequent definitive failure may permit a verified retry, and older in-flight authorization or failure observations cannot overwrite a newer attempt observation.
